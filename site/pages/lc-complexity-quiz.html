Complexity Quiz

Read the Python. State the time and space complexity. Get judged.

Answers are free text — O(n log n), nlogn and N·logN all count, and so does O(n log n + n) — a term another one provably dominates is dropped. Time and space are marked separately, so a half-right answer scores half. Unless a question says otherwise, the space asked for is auxiliary: the returned answer does not count against you. After you check, the lines that set each bound light up in the code.

The snippets are the classics — Blind 75, NeetCode 150 / 250 and LeetCode's Top 100 Liked — plus a handful of pure drills where the shape of the loop is the whole question.
